## ✨ Project Overview

**Chinsuine** is more than a standard restaurant menu—it is an editorial digital dining room and bite-sized encyclopedia celebrating Chinese culinary craft. From breaking down the Sichuan *Mala* Scoville scale to profiling diners based on their dim sum and hotpot orders, the site pairs expressive Google Fonts with a custom circular-photo dark-mode layout.

> **🎯 Goal:** Create a bold, memorable, and clean web experience that combines dark-mode editorial styling with witty culinary storytelling.

## 🔥 Features

- **Editorial Brand Typography:** Pairs calligraphic `Pinyon Script` display branding (`Chinsuine`) with bold `Pixelify Sans` callouts on a sleek `#121212` dark canvas.
- **Alternating Circular Media Layout:** Uses custom `.circle-imageleft` and `.circle-imageright` (`border-radius: 50%`) floats to weave dish photography cleanly alongside story blocks.
- **The Quick Wiki of Our History:** Bite-sized breakdowns covering culinary origin stories, the *Sichuan Mala Scoville Scale*, and *Dim Sum Basket Taxonomy*.
- **Chinese Starter Pack:** Curated first-timer banquet picks (*Soup Dumplings*, *Kung Pao Chicken*, *Peking Roast Duck*, *Dan Dan Noodles*) with optimal ordering notes.
- **How to Eat Dim Sum Like a Pro:** Illustrated table etiquette covering the *Spoon-Nibble Soup Release*, *Lazy Susan Torque Rule*, and *Chopstick Back-End Protocol*.
- **What Your Order Says About You:** Humorous diner personality profiles matched to classic dishes (*Chaos Enthusiast*, *Tactical Perfectionist*, *Nostalgia Loyalist*, *Zen Minimalist*).
- **Takeout Packaging Critique:** Design and thermodynamic improvements for traditional delivery containers (*Vented Crisp-Lock Carton*, *Dual-Compartment Broth & Noodle Bowl*, and *Ergonomic Hook-Lip Soup Spoon*).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Markup:** Semantic HTML5 (`index.html`)
- **Styling:** CSS3 (`style.css` — custom circular image masks, float clearing, and dark-mode theme)
- **Typography:** Google Fonts (`Pinyon Script` and `Pixelify Sans`)
- **Deployment:** GitHub Pages
